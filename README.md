### 🔬 Core Technical Background & Experience

- 🏢 **Scientific ML & Surrogate Modeling @ Mercedes-Benz:**
  - Benchmarked deep learning and ML surrogate architectures (**HistGradientBoosting, MLP, Ridge Regression, CNN, DIMGP**) predicting structural Frequency Response Functions (FRFs) of EV & ICE powertrain components from DOE-based **NASTRAN** simulations.
  - Achieved the highest curve-prediction accuracy on a custom skill score metric with **HistGradientBoosting**, cutting solve times from **~6 hours to seconds**.
- ⚡ **Electric Motor NVH & Structural Dynamics (Master's Thesis) @ Valeo eAutomotive:**
  - Developed optimized FE models in **ANSYS Mechanical** for modal and harmonic response analysis.
  - Extracted natural frequencies of the stator to identify structural resonance against electromagnetic excitation forces (derived from **ANSYS Maxwell** & experimental test bench).
  - Correlated simulation results with physical housing accelerometer measurements; used **FFT / frequency-domain analysis** to isolate dominant vibration & airborne noise modes.
- 🔊 **Vibroacoustics & CAE Automation @ Valeo:**
  - Automated vibroacoustic simulation setups in **Simcenter 3D** using **Python & NX Open API**.
  - Modeled sound sources, acoustic environments, and microphone arrays for airborne noise and ultrasonic sensing; developed custom Python tools for **Sound Pressure Level (SPL)** and acoustic energy field visualization.
- 🏎️ **Composite Manufacturing @ FAU High Voltage Motorsports (Formula Student):**
  - Hands-on composite manufacturing, monocoque chassis fabrication, and CFK layup.
- 🛠️ **Side Projects & Automation:**
  - Build full-stack micro-SaaS applications, asynchronous distributed scrapers (Telethon/MTProto), and LLM workflow integrations.
